4. VALIDITY CHECKS
==========================================================================================
Interpretation of 'net new category units': extra tuna bought at Dominick's that did not
come from other tuna products in the same store-week. It can be higher consumption, earlier
purchases stored at home (no post-promotion dip was measured in Phase 6), or shoppers who
would otherwise have bought tuna at another retailer. Store-level data cannot separate these.

(a) Lead test, reduced form: outcome on this week's AND 2-weeks-later promotion volume.

==========================================================================================
Interpretation of 'net new category units': extra soft-drink bought at Dominick's that did not
come from other soft-drink products in the same store-week. It can be higher consumption, earlier
purchases stored at home (no post-promotion dip was measured in Phase 6), or shoppers who
would otherwise have bought soft-drink at another retailer. Store-level data cannot separate these.

(a) Lead test, reduced form: outcome on this week's AND 2-weeks-later promotion volume.
